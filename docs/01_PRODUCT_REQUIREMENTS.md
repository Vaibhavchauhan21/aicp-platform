# Product Requirements

## Student
- Authentication and profile.
- Technical, soft-skill, and aptitude assessments.
- Skill profile and readiness score.
- Skill-gap analysis against target roles.
- Career recommendations.
- Learning/certification recommendations.
- Internship/job search and personalized matching.
- Application and status tracking.
- AI resume generation and analysis.
- Digital portfolio with skills, projects, certificates, internships, achievements.
- Notifications.

## Industry
- Company profile.
- Post internships, projects, apprenticeships, entry-level jobs.
- Define qualifications and required skills.
- View/filter applicants.
- Compatibility score and explanation.
- Shortlisting and application-status management.
- Publish learning programs and collaboration opportunities.

## Academician
- Profile.
- Faculty internships, industrial training, FDPs.
- Consultancy, research, mentorship, guest lectures, live projects.
- Search, view, apply, and track.

## Institution
- Student overview.
- Skill/readiness analytics.
- Internship and placement analytics.
- Industry engagement.
- Skill-demand trends.

## Admin
- User/company/institution management.
- Opportunity moderation.
- Skill/career/assessment management.
- Verification and platform analytics.
